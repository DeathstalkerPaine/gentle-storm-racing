Beyond the Grid.

GSR Pro Plus is an immersive, narrative-driven racing career mode. It transforms standard track laps into a massive, interconnected journey where your choices on and off the track dictate your progression, your alliances, and your legacy.

📖

The Story Campaign

Advance through episodic chapters. Each race is tied to narrative missions, rivalries, and lore within the GSR universe. Complete chapters to unlock exclusive tracks, vehicles, and titles.

⚖️

Morality & Alignment

Your racing style and narrative choices matter. Are you a clean racer (Good), a ruthless competitor (Bad), or a pragmatic mercenary (Neutral)? Your alignment changes your story outcomes and unlocks different crew bonuses.

⚙️

Dynamic Skill Trees

Earn XP from racing and spend it on your personal Skill Tree. Upgrade your pit crew efficiency, unlock telemetry HUD enhancements, and gain passive buffs for your chosen vehicle class.

🪙

Coins & Progression

Accumulate GSR Coins through race placements and campaign milestones. Use your earnings to purchase elite vehicle liveries, sponsor decals, and premium avatar racing gear from the GSR Store.
