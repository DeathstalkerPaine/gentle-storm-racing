How Systems Interact

🔧 Tuning Points

Tuning points are directly allocated to your vehicle HUD in Second Life. Spending these points allows you to optimize telemetry variables—such as drift physics, acceleration curves, and braking power—giving you a mechanical edge tailored exactly to your driving style.

⏱️ Pit Crew Slots

Endurance races require mandatory pit stops. Unlocking Pit Crew slots and the elite Crew Chief reduces the time you spend frozen on the pit pad, shaving critical seconds off your overall race time. As our economy develops, you'll use Storm Coins to hire and upgrade specific crew members.
