Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about competing, telemetry, and syndicates in Gentle Storm Racing.

Telemetry & Rankings

How is my live telemetry tracked?

GSR utilizes proprietary LSL scripts embedded in track infrastructure and official vehicle HUDs in Second Life. When you cross a finish line, take damage, or set a fastest lap, the data is pushed directly to our Supabase servers and instantly updates your Racer Card.

How are Rating Points (PTS) calculated?

Points are awarded dynamically based on race outcomes, with bonuses for setting track records, maintaining low damage (Defensive bonuses), and aggressive takedowns. Disqualifications (DSQ) penalize your overall rating.

Why does my photo say "Pending"?

To maintain grid immersion and comply with Second Life community standards, all custom driver photos and team logos require manual moderation before they are pushed live to the global telemetry feeds.

Syndicates & Comms

How do I apply for a Team?

Navigate to the Teams Profile page of a recruiting Syndicate. If their roster is open (indicated by a green blinking dot), click "Apply for Roster." Your telemetry will be forwarded directly to the Team Leader for review.

How does the GSR Comms Widget work?

The Comms Widget links your web profile to the live chat database. You can tune your frequency to the Global Feed, your private Team Chat, or listen strictly to racers you follow via the Following channel.

What happens if a Team Leader turns off recruiting?

Once a Team Leader toggles their recruitment status to closed, the application portal on their Team Card locks instantly, and they are removed from the "Free Agent" recruitment boards until they manually reopen their roster.

Leagues & Account Setup

What is the Pro Plus Subscriber League?

Pro Plus is the premier racing tier of GSR. Drivers in this bracket face no vehicle restriction limits, compete for higher point multipliers, and gain access to the exclusive Pro Plus Comms frequency.

Why isn't my Follow button saving?

Following drivers and transmitting in GSR Comms requires an active authentication session. Ensure you have clicked "Sign In" at the top right of the screen. Your session is securely managed via our database backend.

Still Need Assistance?

If your telemetry is out of sync or you are experiencing HUD issues in Second Life, contact a grid official.
