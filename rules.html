Official Grid Code of Conduct

Fair play, telemetry integrity, and mutual respect across Gentle Storm Racing and Second Life sim racing.

Track & Racing Etiquette

1. Clean Racing & Contact

Avoid deliberate ramming, blocking, or reckless driving during official league events. While aggressive overtakes and tactical door-to-door racing are permitted, intentional wrecking will result in telemetry penalties, point deductions, or race disqualification (DSQ).

2. LSL Telemetry Integrity

Simulating or exploiting track infrastructure scripts, HUD timing triggers, or manipulating packet data to artificially alter lap times or damage metrics is strictly prohibited. All violations result in immediate permanent banishment from the global leaderboards.

3. Flag Rules & Marshalling

Drivers must respect track marshals, official restart signals, and grid countdowns in Second Life. Failure to yield during blue-flag situations or unsafe re-entries across racing lines will incur review by league officials.

GSR Comms & Community

4. Respectful Transmissions

Harassment, hate speech, spamming, or toxic behavior across Global, Team, or Pro Plus chat channels will not be tolerated. Keep transmissions professional, competitive, and focused on racing camaraderie.

5. Syndicate & Roster Ethics

Team Leaders and recruiting syndicates must maintain accurate public rosters. Poaching drivers through deceptive messaging or exploiting team application bugs will result in administrative intervention by grid command.

6. Media & Branding Standards

Custom driver photos, logos, and team banners uploaded to profiles must adhere to safe-for-work guidelines and Second Life community standards. Inappropriate imagery will be unannounced and deleted by moderators.

Enforcement & Appeals

7. The Three-Strike System

Minor infractions (chat spam, minor track clipping) result in formal warnings. Repeated safety violations escalate to race suspensions, point wipes, and permanent expulsion from official GSR championships.

8. Dispute Resolution

If you believe a telemetry error, DSQ, or chat sanction was applied incorrectly, file an official review request through the Comms Hub with logged evidence or video capture. Grid officials evaluate protests weekly.

Race Clean. Compete Hard.

Participation in any Gentle Storm Racing event or web portal implies complete agreement with these established rules.
